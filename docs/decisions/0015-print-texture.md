# 0015: Print texture

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-01

## Context

After the layout pass (ADR 0014), Joe's note was "needs texture." The site was flat color on flat color. The references in the brief (B.B. King, Howlin' Wolf, Hendrix) are posters and record sleeves that were printed: screen-printed ink, letterpress wood type, poster stock. Earlier feedback still holds: no gradients, nothing "instagrammy", no gimmicks.

## Decisions

- **Texture means print, made from the palette.** Four tiles, generated by `scripts/make-textures.ts` from a seeded random number generator, so they're ours, seamless and reproducible. Each draws only bone or ink at low alpha. No new colors.
  - `ink.webp`: uneven screen-printed black, faint dust, a few hairline scratches. On the page and the black sections.
  - `paper.webp`: poster stock, with soft mottling, fine fibers and the odd fleck of ink. On the bone booking section, the red mailing list band, and the show-row "ticket" on hover.
  - `letterpress.webp`: a mask for the big display type, with fine voids where worn wood type didn't take the ink. Denser in patches, about 1% of the ink overall. Drawn at 2x so it stays sharp on retina screens.
  - `edge.webp`: a rough paper edge where the bone and red sections begin, as if laid over the black.
- **Restraint was the hard part.** The first draft had big blotchy voids in the type, hair-like fibers and visible scratches. It read as a "distressed font", the gimmick we were avoiding. Every setting came down until the texture shows up close and disappears at a glance.
- **One stylesheet,** `src/styles/texture.css`, so the whole treatment can be tuned or turned off in one place.
- **Off where it gets in the way:** with `prefers-contrast: more`, and on the printed flyer (the paper has its own texture).
- **The film grain stays.** It's fixed to the screen like film; the new textures scroll with the page like a surface. Together they read as a printed page under a projector, not a filter.

## Consequences

- About 38 KB more on the home page (the tiles total 37 KB). Lighthouse is still within budget (home 96, show page 100, press kit 97; accessibility, best practices and SEO 100).
- The letterpress mask is on display type only: never body text, labels or buttons. Contrast checks are unaffected, since the voids remove ink rather than lightening it.
- The torn edges overlap the section above by their full height (28 px), so the page behind never shows through the tear.
